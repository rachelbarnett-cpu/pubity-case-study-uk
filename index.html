Campaign Overview

12 posts live between 14 Jul–18 Aug 2025. Kicked off with a headline post on Pubity (14.07) with 2 weeks of media spend, then BritishMemes grid posts, with stories running throughout across Pubity, Memezar and BritishMemes.

Real Performance

3.3MPubity UK Views (143%)
828KBritishMemes Views (517%!)
1.21%CTR on Pubity
40.5KLink Clicks
97KPoll Votes
11.32%Story Poll ER

What Worked

BritishMemes dramatically overdelivered at 517% of target — UK-native humour resonated perfectly. Adding polls to stories pivoted content from click-delivery to engagement, generating 97K votes. Story share on Pubity delivered 190% to target.

Learnings

1. Use bespoke content focused on activities and adventure (audience uplift data showed these as key associations). 2. Retargeting campaign needed — retargeting engaged users with click-specific assets would have driven better CTR without diluting organic content. 3. Polls > click-through stories — stories drove 11.32% ER with polls vs near-zero without.
